2010

The Social Media Explosion

The 2010s saw social media's explosive growth, turning platforms like Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram into powerful tools that influenced public opinion, shaped cultural trends, and impacted politics. Social media became a primary news source, a business marketplace, and a stage for social movements, with influencers and viral memes altering global connectivity and interaction.

3.8B

By the end of the decade, over 3.8 billion people, or roughly half of the world's population, were using the internet.

Key Moments of the 2010s

  • 2010: Instagram is launched, changing the landscape of social media with its focus on visual content.
  • 2012: Facebook reaches 1 billion active users, solidifying its position as the world's largest social network.
  • 2014: The rise of mobile internet, with mobile devices surpassing desktop computers for internet usage for the first time.
  • 2016: The influence of social media on global events becomes undeniable with the Brexit vote and U.S. presidential election.

The Mobile Revolution

The 2010s were marked by the shift from desktop to mobile internet usage. The proliferation of smartphones and mobile apps transformed how people interact with the web, making it an integral part of everyday life.
