The Pandemic's Impact

The COVID-19 pandemic drastically changed internet usage. Remote work, online education, and digital entertainment became the new norm, leading to unprecedented growth in online activity and innovation.

Rise of Streaming

Streaming services like Netflix, Disney+, and Twitch became central to entertainment, with millions turning to them for content during lockdowns. The decade saw a significant shift from traditional media to on-demand, online content consumption.

2020

4.7B

By 2021, the global number of internet users surpassed 4.66 billion, representing nearly 60% of the world's population.

The Growth of E-commerce

E-commerce saw a massive boost as consumers shifted to online shopping during the pandemic. Companies like Amazon and Shopify expanded rapidly, and small businesses increasingly moved online to survive.

5G Rollout

The 2020s marked the beginning of widespread 5G deployment, promising faster internet speeds, lower latency, and the potential to unlock new technologies like smart cities and advanced IoT applications.

Video Conferencing Boom

Platforms like Zoom and Microsoft Teams saw explosive growth as businesses, schools, and individuals turned to video conferencing for work, learning, and staying connected during lockdowns. This shift highlighted the critical role of the internet in maintaining social and professional ties.
