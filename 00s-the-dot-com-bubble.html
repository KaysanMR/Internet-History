The Dot-Com Crash

  • Market Collapse: The early 2000s were marked by the burst of the dot-com bubble, leading to a significant economic downturn. Many internet startups that had seen rapid growth in the 1990s went bankrupt.
  • Recovery & Innovation: Despite the crash, the internet continued to evolve, with companies like Amazon and Google not only surviving but thriving and setting the stage for the next era of digital innovation.

The Rise of Social Media

The 2000s saw the emergence of social media platforms like Facebook, MySpace, and Twitter, fundamentally changing how people interact online and shaping the digital landscape for years to come.

1B+

By the end of the 2000s, the number of internet users worldwide surpassed 1 billion, a milestone in global connectivity.

2000

Key Moments of the 2000s

  • 2001: Wikipedia is launched, becoming the largest and most popular general reference work on the internet.
  • 2004: Facebook is founded, ushering in the era of social media and changing how people connect online.
  • 2005: YouTube is created, revolutionizing online video sharing and consumption.

The 2000s were a transformative decade for the internet, marked by the rise of social media, the proliferation of broadband, and the increasing integration of the internet into daily life.
