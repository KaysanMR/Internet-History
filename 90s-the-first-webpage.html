1990

Key Moments of the 1990s

  • 1991: The World Wide Web becomes publicly available, revolutionizing how we access information.
  • 1993: The Mosaic browser is launched, making the internet accessible to the masses with a user-friendly interface.
  • 1998: Google is founded, forever changing how we search and find information online.

16M+

By the end of the decade, over 16 million websites existed, compared to just a few thousand at the start of the 1990s.

The Rise of E-Commerce

The 1990s saw the birth of online shopping, with Amazon and eBay launching in 1994 and 1995, respectively. This was a pivotal moment in the history of retail, as businesses began exploring the potential of the internet to reach global audiences.

The Dot-Com Boom

The late 1990s were characterized by the dot-com boom, where internet-based companies saw explosive growth and investment. However, this rapid expansion also led to the dot-com bust in the early 2000s, as many startups failed to turn a profit.

The 1990s laid the groundwork for the internet as we know it today, with rapid technological advancements and the birth of some of the world's most influential companies.
