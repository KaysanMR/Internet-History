About This Website

Welcome to Internet History, a website dedicated to exploring the transformative journey of the internet, from its humble beginnings in the 1960s to its current role as a central part of modern life.


Our goal is to give you a glimpse into the key moments that shaped the digital world we live in today. We cover significant events, milestones, and trends from each decade, illustrating how the internet evolved into what it is today.

  • 1960s: The birth of the internet begins with ARPANET, a network developed for military and academic use.
  • 1990s: The World Wide Web and commercial opportunities bring the internet to the masses.
  • 2010s: Social media and mobile technology reshape how people communicate and consume information.

From early connections between a handful of computers to a global network connecting billions of people, the internet's history is rich and complex. Through this website, we aim to show the rapid technological changes that have occurred and their impact on society.
